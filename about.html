Our Story

Two centuries of craft, reimagined

For over 200 years, our family has been rooted in the world of gold and diamond jewellery, a legacy that began in Agra. Today it lives on in Venora Jewels: our pieces are manufactured in our own factory in Surat, India's diamond city, and the brand was born in Noida, where we display our collections. What began as a legacy of artistry has become a modern reflection of luxury, ethics and elegance.

At Venora, our vision is simple yet powerful: to provide ethical, sustainable lab grown diamond jewellery to everyone, without stretching budgets. We believe that true luxury is not about exclusivity, but about inclusivity — bringing brilliance within reach of all.

Beyond adornment, we see jewellery as a statement of empowerment — crafted to celebrate and empower every woman with the finest diamonds. With Venora Jewels, you don't just wear jewellery. You carry a legacy, embrace modern values, and own a piece of luxury made for everyone.

Craftsmanship

Made by hand, made to last

Each Venora piece is manufactured in our own factory in Surat by skilled artisans, drawing on two centuries of family expertise. Diamonds are selected for their cut and brilliance, then set in hallmarked 9KT, 14KT or 18KT gold.

Before it reaches you, every piece is inspected for symmetry, security of settings and finish — so it looks beautiful on day one and for years to come.
