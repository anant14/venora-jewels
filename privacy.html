Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

How Venora Jewels collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Who we are

Venora Jewels is a lab grown diamond jewellery brand based in Noida, India, with manufacturing in Surat. You can reach us at or on WhatsApp at .

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Required: your name, mobile number and email address (from your Google account).
  • Optional: your gender, age, date of birth and anniversary date — only if you choose to share them.
  • The date you joined and when you last logged in.

When you send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email, that message is handled by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own privacy policies.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and give you access to our catalogue.
  • To respond to your enquiries and contact you about designs you are interested in.
  • If you share your birthday or anniversary, to send you wishes and occasional offers around those dates.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Where it is stored

Your account details are stored securely using Google Firebase. Only you and authorised Venora Jewels staff can see your details.

Your choices

  • You can view and edit your details at any time on the My Account page.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and details by emailing or messaging us. We will do so within 30 days.
  • You can ask us to stop sending you offers at any time.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
