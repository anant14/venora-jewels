Step 1 · Calibrate

Match a bank card

Screens differ in size, so first hold any bank/ID card (standard 85.6 mm) against the screen and move the slider until the gold box is exactly as wide as your card.

Hold your card here
Step 2 · Measure

Place a ring you own

Put a ring that fits you well on the circle and adjust until the circle matches the inside edge of the ring.

No ring? No problem

Measure with a thread

  1. Wrap a thread or thin paper strip around the base of your finger — snug, but not tight.
  2. Mark the point where it overlaps with a pen.
  3. Measure the length up to the mark with a ruler in millimetres.
  4. Find that number below in the size chart (circumference column).

✦ Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are slightly larger.

✦ Make sure it can slide over your knuckle.

✦ Between two sizes? Choose the larger one — and for wide bands, go up half a size.

✦ Unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp — we can courier a free ring sizer.
