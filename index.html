The Science of Sparkle

What are lab grown diamonds?

Lab grown diamonds are genuine diamonds, with the same carbon composition as naturally mined diamonds. They are created using advanced technology and are structurally, optically and chemically identical to mined diamonds.

They are not American diamonds, zirconia or moissanite. By choosing a lab grown diamond, you don't compromise on quality or beauty — only on price.
